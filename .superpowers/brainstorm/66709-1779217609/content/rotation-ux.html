Rotation UX During Placement

When you have a piece selected and are hovering it over the scene to place, how should rotation work?

A

R key cycles (current) + scroll wheel rotates

Keep the existing R key for 90° increments. Also let the mouse scroll wheel rotate while in place mode. Ghost updates live as you rotate.

B

Q / E keys for rotation (no camera pan)

Q and E currently pan the camera. Reassign them to rotate the placement piece (Q = −90°, E = +90°). Camera panning moves to middle-mouse or another combo. Clean, game-like feel.

C

Both: R / scroll wheel rotate, Q/E stay on camera

R key and scroll wheel both rotate the ghost piece. Q and E remain on camera pan. Two input paths for rotation means you never have to take hands off mouse.
